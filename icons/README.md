# EarBy icon history

Full-resolution sources for the icons kept in this snapshot. Only `EarByIconV6.png` is current;
`EarByIconV5.png` is the fallback. Neither is packaged as a source image: the app and store use the
PNG sizes generated from V6.

| File | Note | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EarByIconV5.png` | Rendered from `design/earby-icon.svg`, CC BY 4.0 | Fallback source |
| `EarByIconV6.png` | Hand-drawn by **Dr Ham Hamster**, used with her permission | **Current launcher and store icon** |

`EarByIconV6.png` is the artwork as delivered. The Android launcher sizes, the adaptive-icon
layers and `store/icon-512.png` are generated from it by `tools/icon/generate_launcher_icons.py`.
The artist retains her rights to the drawing; it is not relicensed under CC BY 4.0 or Apache-2.0.
See `docs/legal/ASSET_SOURCES.md` for the recorded permission and attribution requirements.

Earlier drafts (`EarByIconV1.png` through `EarByIconV4.png`) are private development history: they
are not part of this snapshot and are not shipped in the app or store listing. V4 is Qwen-Image
2.1-derived and carries a non-commercial licence; see `docs/legal/ASSET_SOURCES.md`.
