EarBy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

EarBy is an offline ear-training app published by the EarBy project.

Information we collect

EarBy does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash reporting, cloud storage, runtime text-to-speech or INTERNET permission. It does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos or files.

Information stored on your device

Mode, setup, volume, appearance and five preset slots are stored in app-private storage. They are not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes this data. Android automatic backup is disabled for EarBy. Preset export writes only the file you choose through Android's system picker.

Children

EarBy is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children.

Third-party services

The app uses no third-party advertising, analytics or cloud services. Bundled spoken answers are prerecorded; no speech service receives your voice or text.
