## Full description

EarBy is passive ear training for the moments when your hands are busy. It plays short musical
examples on your phone and then speaks the answer, so you build recognition by listening rather
than tapping.

Nine training modes cover interval recognition, interval walks, chords, scales, note
identification, scale degrees, chord progressions, intervals in context and melodic dictation.
Each mode supports Recognition, Exposure and Association learning, its own setup and five presets.

Everything is generated on the device from bundled sounds and prerecorded English answers. EarBy
has no account, no ads, no analytics, no network permission and no runtime text-to-speech. Your
settings and presets stay on your phone.

Use the sleep timer for hands-off practice. Choose the examples and timing that fit your ear, then
listen.
