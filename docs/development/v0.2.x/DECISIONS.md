### D08 — Equivalent chord answers: ToneDear procedure

Owner response on 2026-10-11: “follow the procedures on https://tonedear.com”. Resolved by
inspecting its public chord exercise and client script; see [reference procedure](REFERENCE_PROCEDURES.md).
Accept equivalent selected chord/inversion interpretations of the sound. Do not insert a
root cue or reject an otherwise feasible setup just because its labels are equivalent.

Passive-app adaptation: compose one answer listing the distinct equivalent answers, joined
by “or”, in stable catalog order. Quality-only answers deduplicate quality names; quality+
inversion answers preserve valid pairs. Include the whole answer in duration validation.
This spoken adaptation is a design refinement, not a claim that ToneDear speaks answers.

### D09 — Scale fragments shared by different scales

Owner response on 2026-10-11: “follow the procedures on https://tonedear.com”. Its scale
exercise uses full scales, with no fragment option in the inspected page/script. M6 therefore
ships complete octave traversals and defers fragments, superseding that part of the original
overall plan and WP5.3. There is no fragment-ambiguity setting to implement.

Retain the project's thirteen-scale vocabulary and explicit both-directions extension;
ToneDear supplies the exercise procedure, not a request to replace every existing product
decision with its catalog or interactive quiz. See [reference procedure](REFERENCE_PROCEDURES.md).

## When the implementing agent must ask

Ask for a pivotal product change, data-loss decision, change to the accepted musical
vocabulary, inability to preserve existing behavior, or a replacement asset/voice with
materially different rights or delivery. Present the concrete alternatives and evidence.

Resolve routine code organization, test fixtures, error wording and scoped repairs using
the contracts. Do not ask again about D01–D09 or pause between authorized packages merely
because a human acceptance row remains open. Never treat elapsed time as approval.
