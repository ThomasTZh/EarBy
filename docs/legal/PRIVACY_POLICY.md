# EarBy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

EarBy is an offline ear-training app published by the EarBy project. This policy explains what the
app does with information.

## Information we collect

**EarBy does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.**

The app has no account system, analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash-reporting service, cloud
storage, runtime text-to-speech or INTERNET permission. It does not request access to contacts,
location, microphone, camera, photos or files.

## Information stored on your device

EarBy stores your selected mode, setup values, volume and appearance settings, and your five preset
slots in app-private storage. These values stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. Uninstalling
the app removes this app-private data. Android automatic backup is disabled for EarBy.

If you choose to export presets, the app writes only the file you choose through Android's system
file picker. That file is under your control.

## Children

EarBy is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly
collect information from children.

## Third-party services

EarBy does not use third-party advertising, analytics or cloud services. The bundled spoken answers
are prerecorded assets; no speech service receives your voice or text.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published at this page with a new effective date.
The app version and release notes will identify the change.
