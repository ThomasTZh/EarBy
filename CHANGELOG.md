# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — release candidate

- Nine training modes, including the finite Interval walk.
- Three learning modes: Recognition, Exposure and Association.
- Five presets and a starter for every mode.
- Offline generated audio and prerecorded spoken answers.
- No account, ads, analytics, telemetry or INTERNET permission.
- Release configuration for a signed Play AAB and standalone APK.
- Preset export/import for local backup.
- New hand-drawn launcher and store icon by **Dr Ham Hamster**, used with her permission. The
  earlier Qwen-Image 2.1 draft is not included.
- Asset provenance for the remaining original and third-party material.
- Public source snapshot at <https://github.com/ThomasTZh/EarBy>: one initial commit, sanitised
  paths and no internal development records; identity stays 1.0.0 / `versionCode` 100.
- License, privacy and third-party notices.
