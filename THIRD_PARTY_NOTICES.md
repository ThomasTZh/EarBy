uses runtime TTS or the network. The following attributions are required and must remain in the
public notices and in-app legal page:

- `en_GB-alba-medium` — dataset CC BY 4.0, University of Edinburgh DataShare,
  https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270. Piper voice card:
  https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices.
- `en_US-amy-medium` — HiFi-TTS / OpenSLR SLR109, CC BY 4.0, via the Piper voice collection.
- `en_GB-cori-high` — LibriVox public-domain recordings.
- `en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium` — voice “**Jenny (Dioco)**”; dataset terms permit commercial use with
  attribution, https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset.
- **Piper engine** — MIT, https://github.com/rhasspy/piper. Individual model cards may add terms;

The generated WAVs are distributed as part of EarBy under CC BY 4.0 for the project’s editing and
packaging, while the upstream dataset/model terms above continue to apply. Do not present the
voices as the project’s exclusive original recordings.
